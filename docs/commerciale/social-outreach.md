**Primi 7 post di lancio (EN)**

1. *Giorno 1 — Luigi (dopo il benvenuto)*
> Let's get this started the useful way. 👇
> Tell us: when are you coming to Puglia, and what's the trip — beaches, towns, food, a bit of everything?
> Drop it in the comments and we'll throw you the first tip to build around. No question too small. 🇮🇹

2. *Giorno 1 — Domenico*
> In the heart of Bari Vecchia there's a narrow street where, every morning, the women sit at their doorsteps making orecchiette by hand — dragging the dough with a knife, flipping it on a thumb, faster than you can watch.
> No sign, no show. It's just been happening there for generations.
> That's the Bari most people walk past without knowing what they're seeing. 🍝
> Have you been to Bari Vecchia — or is it on your list?

3. *Giorno 2 — Domenico*
> Quick one. Why is half of Bari named after Saint Nicholas? 🎅
> Because in 1087 sailors from Bari sailed to modern-day Turkey and brought back the relics of San Nicola — yes, that Nicholas, the one who became Santa Claus. The whole Basilica was built to house them.
> So when you walk into Bari, you're walking into the hometown of Father Christmas. Most visitors have no idea.
> What's a "wait, really?" fact that made a place click for you?

4. *Giorno 2 — Luigi (poll)*
> Settle a debate for us. ⚖️
> You've got one afternoon on the coast south of Bari. Where do you spend it?
> 🔵 Polignano a Mare — the cliffs, the cove, the drama
> ⚪ Monopoli — the harbour, the old town, fewer crowds
> Vote 🔵 or ⚪ and tell us why.

5. *Giorno 3 — Domenico*
> Walk through Bari at 8am and you'll smell it before you see it: focaccia barese, straight from the oven, the top dimpled with cherry tomatoes and olives, the bottom crisp from the olive oil and a little semolina.
> Locals eat it standing, folded, before lunch is even a thought.
> It's not a snack here. It's a small daily ritual. 🫒
> What's the one food you'd travel to Puglia just to eat?

6. *Giorno 3 — Luigi (tip)*
> Planning tip people always thank us for later: don't try to "do" all of Puglia in one trip. 🗺️
> The towns are spread out and half the magic is slowing down. A relaxed route:
> • Bari + the coast (Polignano, Monopoli)
> • Valle d'Itria (Alberobello, Locorotondo, Ostuni)
> • Matera as a day-or-two add-on
> 5–7 days, one or two bases, no daily packing.
> How many days are you giving Puglia?

7. *Giorno 4 — Domenico (storia + soft prodotto)*
> In Polignano a Mare, on a cliff over the sea, there's a man born in 1928 named Domenico Modugno. You don't know the name? You know the song: "Volare" — he wrote it. There's a statue of him with his arms wide open, facing the water that raised him.
> Standing there, the song stops being a cliché and starts making sense.
> These are the bits we love telling — the why behind a place. It's what we built Localis to do: guides explain, we tell the story. If you want one of these in your ear while you walk Polignano, just ask. 🎧
> Which Puglia town do you most want the story behind?
