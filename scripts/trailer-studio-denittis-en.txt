Part 1 (title, not read)
Look at this garden. A woman, a child, breakfast in the morning light. The leaves filter the sun, and time seems to stand still. It's Léontine, the wife of Giuseppe De Nittis. And their son, Jacques. He paints them here, in the courtyard of their home in Paris — in the calm of an ordinary day. He doesn't know it's one of the last.

Part 2 (title, not read)
A year later, at thirty-eight, a sudden stroke takes him. This is what remains: the happiness of one morning, held forever on canvas. De Nittis was born in Barletta. And to Barletta he would return — in one hundred and thirty-eight paintings, given by his Léontine. Look again. Some things, you don't want to let go.
